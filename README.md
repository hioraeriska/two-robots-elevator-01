## Why this line works

The joke is built from the situation itself: two machines stuck in a small
box, forced to do the most human thing there is — fill silence. The first
half sounds competent, a robot that rehearsed the ride eleven times, and the
second half quietly admits the rehearsal was useless, because the thing it
cannot solve is where to put arms it does not even have. That is the
awkwardness the brief asks for: a machine applying careful logic to a
problem that was never a problem, and losing to it anyway. It reads like
something said out loud to the only other robot in the lift, not like a joke
written about robots from the outside.
